Industries

Ten sectors. One common language of bankability.

Digital infrastructure is no longer just telecom. It spans the physical and computational layers that power modern economies.

Telecommunications

Fixed and mobile operators expanding coverage, upgrading networks and structuring next-generation licensing and spectrum strategies.

Fiber Networks

Backbone and last-mile fiber deployment — route economics, rights-of-way, open-access models and financing structures.

Mobile Networks

4G/5G rollout planning, tower and RAN sharing structures, and regulatory strategy for national mobile coverage.

Data Centers

Hyperscale and edge data center siting, power availability, financing and public-sector co-location strategy.

Cloud Infrastructure

Sovereign and commercial cloud strategy, digital public infrastructure hosting, and cloud procurement structuring.

Satellite Systems

LEO/GEO connectivity strategy, spectrum coordination and satellite-terrestrial hybrid network structuring.

AI Infrastructure

Compute capacity planning, power and cooling strategy, and public-private structuring for national AI infrastructure.

Submarine Cables

Route planning, landing station strategy, capacity offtake structuring and multi-country consortium financing.

Smart Cities

Digital public services, IoT infrastructure and integrated urban connectivity strategy.

Energy

Power availability strategy for digital infrastructure, and structuring of energy-digital co-investment.

Representative Engagements

The kind of programs INA is built to structure.

A representative cross-section of the digital infrastructure programs INA advises governments and regulators on — spanning financing design, network deployment and regulatory modernization.

Universal Service Fund Strengthening

Expanding, restructuring or leveraging national universal service funds to close connectivity gaps in underserved areas.

Digital Infrastructure Fund Design

Designing and structuring dedicated funds to channel investment into digital and technology infrastructure.

Regional Data & AI Center Networks

National networks of regional data centers and AI compute infrastructure, sited and financed across federal territory.

Fixed Wireless Access Deployment

National programs for FWA site rollout, extending broadband coverage where fiber isn’t yet viable.

4G/5G Coverage Expansion

Mobile site deployment programs targeting areas with no coverage or persistently poor service.

Public Interest Connectivity

Connectivity for border posts, health centers and other sites where coverage serves a clear public interest.

Critical Digital Infrastructure

Data centers, fiber networks and submarine cable redundancy strategy for national digital resilience.

Digital Rail Corridors

National programs deploying fiber optic backbone along existing rail rights-of-way.

Shared & Open-Access Infrastructure

National passive infrastructure-sharing and open-access programs to lower deployment costs and speed rollout.

Regulatory Modernization

Technology adoption and process simplification inside telecom regulators, improving service to operators and the public.

Program design typically includes the financing strategy itself — whether drawing on a universal service fund or structuring external financing through multilateral development finance institutions.
